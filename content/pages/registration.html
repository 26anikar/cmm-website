Caltech Math Meet 2027 will take place on January 30th, 2027.

Registration is open from now until November 13th.

In-Person Registration

Registration for CMM 2026 is now open through Friday, November 13th.

CMM's in-person competition consists of the following:

  • Team Round (in-person)

  • Guts Round (in-person) (note that we have replaced Power with Guts due to AI reasons)

  • Individual Round (in-person)

  • All other optional in-person events (Integration Bee, Admissions event, Math talk)

  • Breakfast and Lunch will be provided, as well as t-shirts and other goods from our sponsors.

We ask that, if possible, a coach, that is a legal guardian, fill out the sign up for each team, and accompany the students of the team to the competition if possible.

If your team is selected, then payment will be due by December 1st. Each team will cost $120 and each individual costs $20. Payment instructions are here.

The top 10 teams from CMM 2026 will be guaranteed spots, but they still must register before the deadline. They are:

AlphaStar Academy: 3 Teams
Random Math: 2 Teams

University High School: 1 Team
Gunn Math Circle: 1 Team
The Bishop's School: 1 Team
