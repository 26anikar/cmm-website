Register for CMM 2027

Caltech Math Meet 2027 will take place on January 23rd, 2027.

Registration is now closed.

The registration form linked below is closed to all teams and individuals who have not been accepted. Teams and individuals who were accepted have through the end of December to edit their registration information, including team rosters, t-shirt sizes, lunch orders, and coach information.

Registration ›

CMM 2027 consists of the following rounds, all occurring in-person.

  • Team Round (olympiad-style)
  • Guts Round (note that this has replaced the Power Round)
  • Individual Round
  • All other optional events (Integration Bee, Admissions event, Math talk)
  • Breakfast and Lunch will be provided, as well as t-shirts and other goods from our sponsors.

You can read more about the rounds of the competition here.

We ask that, if possible, a coach, that is a legal guardian, fill out the sign up for each team, and accompany the students of the team to the competition if possible.

Payment for accepted teams and individuals is due by December 1st. Each team will cost $120 and each individual costs $20. Payment instructions are here.

Questions? Contact Uscmm-help@caltech.edu
